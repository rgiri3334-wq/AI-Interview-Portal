Sterling E-Mobility Solutions Limited (SEM) — formerly Sterling Gtake E-Mobility — is India's
largest manufacturer of Motor Control Units (MCUs) for electric vehicles, a 100% subsidiary of
Sterling Tools Limited (BSE/NSE listed). Incorporated on January 10, 2020, SEM has rapidly
evolved from a single-product MCU supplier into a comprehensive EV powertrain and power
electronics solutions provider with 720,000 units/year manufacturing capacity and over 50%
market share in India's MCU segment.

Sterling Tools Limited established an E-Mobility Division in 2018 with the objective of
supporting Auto OEMs with leading-edge technology products in automotive electronics and
electric vehicle domains. The subsidiary was incorporated in 2020 to:
1. Capitalize on India's EV transition under FAME II and growing EV adoption
2. Leverage Sterling Group's 40+ years of automotive component manufacturing expertise
