## 1.1 Introduction

Sterling E-Mobility Solutions Limited (SEM), formerly known as Sterling Gtake E-Mobility Limited, stands as India's largest manufacturer of Motor Control Units (MCUs) for electric vehicles, commanding a dominant market share exceeding 50% in the domestic MCU segment. Operating as a 100% wholly-owned subsidiary of Sterling Tools Limited — a publicly listed company on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE: 532404) and the National Stock Exchange (NSE: STERTOOLS) — SEM represents the most significant strategic pivot in Sterling Group's four-decade-long journey in automotive component manufacturing.

Incorporated on January 10, 2020, under the Corporate Identification Number (CIN) U31909DL2020PLC360123, the company has evolved at a remarkable pace — from a single-product Motor Control Unit supplier operating under a technology licensing model, to a comprehensive Electric Vehicle (EV) powertrain and power electronics solutions provider with an installed manufacturing capacity of 720,000 MCUs per year. This transformation has been achieved within a span of just five years, reflecting not only the exceptional execution capability of its leadership but also the company's astute understanding of India's rapidly accelerating EV transition.

The organisation's dual operational presence — a state-of-the-art manufacturing campus in Faridabad, Haryana, and a dedicated Research & Development and Application Engineering technical centre in Bengaluru, Karnataka — enables SEM to simultaneously deliver large-scale production volumes while maintaining the engineering rigour required to serve India's most demanding EV Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs). This geographical and functional duality is a hallmark of SEM's engineering-led identity, encapsulated in its official mission statement: *"To be an Engineering-led Company providing Innovative Solutions as well as best-in-class Application & After-Sales Support to the EV Industry."*

This chapter presents a comprehensive organisational overview of Sterling E-Mobility Solutions Limited, encompassing its founding history, corporate evolution, manufacturing footprint, complete product portfolio, technology partnerships, financial performance, leadership structure, and strategic future roadmap. This background is essential context for understanding the imperative that drove the development of the Sterling Intelligent Interview & Candidate Assessment Platform — the subject of this project report.

## 1.2 Parent Company — Sterling Tools Limited

### 1.2.1 Corporate Heritage

To fully appreciate Sterling E-Mobility's trajectory, one must first understand the institutional foundation from which it emerged. Sterling Tools Limited was founded in 1979 by Mr. Anil Aggarwal, currently Chairman of the Sterling Group. Over its four-decade history, Sterling Tools Limited established itself as one of India's premier manufacturers of cold-forged high-tensile fasteners for the automotive industry, supplying to leading automobile OEMs across passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, and two-wheelers.

The company's listing on both the BSE and NSE provided it with the financial discipline, investor accountability, and governance structures that would later prove invaluable when steering its strategic entry into the EV components domain. Sterling Tools Limited's consolidated revenue for FY2025 stood at ₹1,038 crore, representing a year-on-year growth of 10.6%, with a Profit After Tax (PAT) of ₹58.3 crore and an EBITDA margin of 12.8%. The declaration of a 200% dividend in FY2025 reflects the robust financial health and strong cash generation of the parent entity.

### 1.2.2 Strategic Entry into E-Mobility

The genesis of Sterling E-Mobility lies in a forward-looking strategic decision made in 2018, when Sterling Tools Limited established a dedicated E-Mobility Division within its corporate structure. This decision, championed by Managing Director Atul Aggarwal, predated the formal incorporation of the EV subsidiary by two years — a testament to the deliberate and calculated nature of the Group's entry into the electric vehicle domain.

The E-Mobility Division was created with three primary strategic objectives:

1. To capitalise on India's EV transition catalysed by the FAME II (Faster Adoption and Manufacture of Hybrid and Electric Vehicles) policy framework introduced by the Government of India.
2. To leverage Sterling Group's 40-plus years of automotive component manufacturing expertise and established OEM relationships in the EV components domain.
3. To achieve import substitution by developing domestic MCU manufacturing capability at scale — a segment where India lacked sufficient indigenous production at the time.

This Division served as the organisational incubator and proof-of-concept vehicle that eventually crystallised into the incorporation of Sterling Gtake E-Mobility Limited in January 2020.

## 1.3 Company Founding and Corporate Evolution

### 1.3.1 Incorporation and Initial Vision

Sterling Gtake E-Mobility Limited was formally incorporated on **January 10, 2020**, as a 100% subsidiary of Sterling Tools Limited. The founding promoters included Atul Aggarwal (Managing Director), Anish Agarwal (Director), and the Sterling Group. The company's original vision was unambiguous: *"We aim to be India's leading manufacturer and supplier of Electric Vehicle Components & Solutions."*

The company's initial business model was built on a technology licensing agreement with **Jiangsu Gtake Electric Co. Ltd.**, a Shenzhen-based Chinese electric motor drive and control technology company. Under this arrangement, SEM obtained exclusive rights to manufacture and market Motor Control Units in India using Gtake's proprietary technology, enabling rapid market entry without the multi-year investment required for indigenous MCU technology development.

The original business model comprised:
- License-based manufacturing of Motor Control Units covering low voltage (48V) to high voltage (700V) applications.
- B2B supply to EV OEMs across the 2-Wheeler (2W), 3-Wheeler (3W), Light Commercial Vehicle (LCV), and Commercial Vehicle segments.
- Differentiated application support and vehicle integration services that competitors could not easily replicate.

### 1.3.2 Rebranding and Strategic Repositioning

In October 2025, the company underwent a significant rebranding exercise, changing its name from **Sterling Gtake E-Mobility Limited** to **Sterling E-Mobility Solutions Limited**. This rebranding was not merely a cosmetic exercise; it represented a fundamental strategic repositioning of the company's identity. The removal of "Gtake" from the corporate name signalled the company's intent to evolve beyond its MCU-centric origins and the exclusive Gtake technology partnership, towards a broader, more autonomous identity as a comprehensive EV powertrain and power electronics solutions provider.

The rebranding coincided with the announcement of new technology partnerships with **Advanced Electric Machines (AEM)** of Washington, UK — for magnet-free traction motor technology — and **Landworld Technology Co. Ltd.** of Shenzhen, China — for On-Board Chargers (OBCs), DC-DC Converters, and Power Distribution Units (PDUs). These partnerships collectively signified SEM's transition from a single-technology licensing model to a diversified, multi-partner technology ecosystem, laying the groundwork for its ambitious FY2028 strategic objectives.

## 1.5 Manufacturing Presence and Infrastructure

### 1.5.1 Faridabad Manufacturing Campus

The primary manufacturing facility of Sterling E-Mobility is located at **12/2, NH-19, Sector 27, Faridabad, Haryana – 121003**. Commonly referred to internally as the "EV Campus," this facility serves as the nerve centre of SEM's production operations, housing:

- **MCU Assembly Lines**: Capable of producing up to 720,000 Motor Control Units per year, with capacity described as expandable to accommodate future demand. The high throughput is supported by semi-automated assembly processes, reflecting the company's investment in manufacturing technology.
- **Testing Infrastructure**: The campus features dynamometer (dyno) testing capability up to 1,200V, enabling in-house validation of MCU performance across the full voltage spectrum of the company's product portfolio. This in-house testing infrastructure is a strategic differentiator, significantly reducing time-to-market for new product variants.
- **OBC/DC-DC Production Line**: As announced in 2025, the Faridabad EV Campus is being expanded to accommodate the manufacturing of On-Board Chargers and DC-DC Converters under the Landworld technology partnership — marking the facility's evolution from an MCU-only plant to a comprehensive powertrain component production centre.

The geographical location of the Faridabad plant — situated on National Highway 19, in close proximity to Delhi NCR's automotive OEM cluster — provides significant logistics advantages in terms of component delivery timelines and OEM proximity.

### 1.5.2 Bengaluru Technical Centre

SEM's second key operational location is its **Technical Centre** in Bengaluru, Karnataka, located at **73, 74, 113, 114, ND Fusion Mall, 1st Phase, BTM Layout, Bengaluru – 560076**. This centre serves as the company's intellectual and application engineering hub, fulfilling several critical functions:

- **Research & Development**: Engineering teams at the Bengaluru centre work on software customisation, calibration, and new product development in collaboration with technology partners.
- **Application Support**: Vehicle integration engineers collaborate directly with OEM clients to integrate SEM's MCUs and powertrain components into customer vehicle platforms, providing system-level validation and troubleshooting.
- **Software Engineering**: Development of CAN-BUS communication protocols, UDS (Unified Diagnostic Services) software, and embedded system calibration — capabilities that differentiate SEM from commodity MCU suppliers.
- **Talent Hub**: Bengaluru's position as India's leading engineering and technology talent cluster makes this location ideal for attracting embedded systems, power electronics, and software engineering talent.

The synergistic relationship between the Faridabad manufacturing campus and the Bengaluru technical centre exemplifies SEM's dual-capability model: world-class manufacturing at scale combined with domain-specific engineering excellence.

## 1.6 Complete Product Portfolio and EV Ecosystem

### 1.6.1 Motor Control Units (MCUs) — Flagship Product

The Motor Control Unit remains the foundational and defining product of Sterling E-Mobility's portfolio. MCUs function as the "brain" of electric vehicles, governing the precise operation of traction motors by controlling torque, speed, and efficiency through sophisticated field-oriented control (FOC) algorithms.

**Table 1.2 — SEM MCU Product Specifications**

| Attribute | Specification |
|---|---|
| Voltage Range | 48V (low voltage) to 700V (high voltage) |
| Power Range | 1 kW to 200 kW |
| Motor Types Supported | BLDC (Brushless DC), PMSM (Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor), Induction Motors |
| Target Segments | 2-Wheeler, 3-Wheeler, LCV (>2.5T), Passenger Car (BEV), Commercial Vehicles |
| Annual Capacity | 720,000 units (expandable) |
| India Market Share | 50%+ (largest domestic MCU manufacturer) |
| FAME II Compliance | Yes — FAME II-compliant MCUs, partially localised |
| Competitive Advantage | India's largest MCU manufacturer; extensive voltage/power portfolio; premium application support |

### 1.6.2 Traction Motors

In line with its evolution into a full powertrain solutions provider, SEM has expanded into traction motor manufacturing and supply. The motor portfolio encompasses:

- **Brushless DC (BLDC) Motors**: Widely used in 2-Wheeler and 3-Wheeler applications for their high efficiency and low maintenance characteristics.
- **Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motors (PMSM)**: Preferred for higher-power LCV and passenger car applications where torque density and efficiency are paramount.
- **Induction Motors (IM)**: Robust, cost-effective motors for commercial vehicle and light industrial EV applications.
- **Magnet-Free Motors**: Perhaps the most strategically significant addition to SEM's motor portfolio — developed through the partnership with Advanced Electric Machines (AEM) of Washington, UK. These motors eliminate the dependency on rare-earth magnets, addressing one of the EV industry's most pressing supply chain vulnerabilities and cost escalation risks.

### 1.6.3 On-Board Chargers (OBC)

SEM's entry into the EV charging segment through its partnership with Landworld Technology has significantly broadened the company's addressable market. The OBC portfolio currently includes:

| Product | Power | Voltage Range | Efficiency | Cooling | Target Application |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6.6 kW OBC | 6.6 kW | Standard range | — | — | Passenger BEV, 2W premium |
| 11 kW OBC | 11 kW | Standard range | — | — | LCV (>2.5T), Passenger BEV |
| 22 kW OBC | 22 kW | 200–750 Vdc | ≥94% | Liquid-cooled | High-power passenger cars, LCV |

The 22 kW OBC, with its liquid-cooled design and ≥94% efficiency rating, is positioned for the premium EV segment where fast on-board charging and high voltage compatibility (up to 750 Vdc) are critical requirements.

### 1.6.4 DC-DC Converters

DC-DC Converters are essential power electronics components in EVs, responsible for stepping down high-voltage battery power to low-voltage auxiliary systems (12V/24V networks for lighting, infotainment, and control systems). SEM's DC-DC Converter portfolio currently includes 2.5 kW and 6 kW variants targeting passenger cars and LCVs.

### 1.6.5 Integrated Powertrain Systems

The most strategically significant product category in SEM's expanding portfolio is its line of Integrated Powertrain Systems — multi-function drive units that combine multiple power electronics components into a single, compact housing:

| System | Components | Value Proposition |
|---|---|---|
| 2-in-1 System | OBC (6.6 kW/11 kW) + DC-DC Converter (2.5 kW) | Space and cost reduction vs. discrete components |
| 3-in-1 System | OBC (6.6 kW/11 kW) + DC-DC Converter (2.5 kW) + PDU | Complete integrated power module; premium positioning |

These integrated systems represent the cornerstone of SEM's FY2028 strategic goal — achieving 70% of portfolio revenue from integrated powertrain systems (up from approximately 30% in 2025).

### 1.6.6 Off-Board Chargers and PDUs

Rounding out the product portfolio are wall-mounted Off-Board Chargers for EV charging station infrastructure and Power Distribution Units (PDUs) for high-voltage power distribution management within EV platforms.

## 1.13 Why Sterling E-Mobility Needs an Intelligent Recruitment System

The organisational narrative of Sterling E-Mobility, as detailed throughout this chapter, converges on a single strategic imperative: **talent**. Every dimension of SEM's ambition — from achieving 720,000-unit annual production capacity, to commercialising magnet-free motor technology, to executing the FY2028 integrated systems strategy — is fundamentally dependent on the quality and velocity of its engineering talent acquisition.

The company operates in a domain where a poorly designed MCU can cause catastrophic vehicle failure; where a software calibration error can result in motor burnout or battery damage; and where embedded systems engineers must simultaneously master field-oriented control theory, CAN-BUS communication protocols, ISO 26262 automotive safety standards, and real-time operating system development. This is not a context where generic, surface-level interviews are adequate.

The specific talent acquisition challenges confronting SEM include:

1. **High Technical Bar**: The company requires engineers with specialised expertise in EV powertrain systems, power electronics, embedded software, and automotive communication protocols — a talent pool that is scarce by definition.
2. **Rapid Scaling Requirement**: With 30 additional OEMs in the commercial pipeline and a manufacturing capacity expansion underway, SEM must accelerate its hiring velocity without compromising the technical quality of candidates admitted to the organisation.
3. **Engineering Bandwidth Constraints**: Senior engineers at SEM — the CTO, application engineers, embedded software leads — are too strategically critical to deploy as first-round interviewers for every applicant. Their time must be protected for product development and OEM support.
4. **Evaluation Consistency**: Without a standardised assessment framework, the quality of candidate evaluation inevitably varies with the interviewer, introducing subjectivity and inconsistency into what should be an objective technical gatekeeping process.
5. **Geographic Distribution**: SEM's dual-location operational model (Faridabad manufacturing + Bengaluru R&D) creates timezone and scheduling complexity for coordinating interview panels.

It is precisely to address these challenges that the **Sterling Intelligent Interview & Candidate Assessment Platform** was conceived, designed, and implemented. The platform automates the early-stage technical screening process, enabling SEM to assess large volumes of candidates with rigorous, consistent, AI-driven technical evaluation — ensuring that only the highest calibre of talent is recommended to the final human interview round.

This project represents not merely an academic exercise in enterprise software development, but a direct response to a genuine operational need of one of India's most dynamic EV technology companies — a company that, as this chapter has demonstrated, is at the forefront of India's electric mobility revolution.
